About

Research-grade ideas, production-grade systems.

I care about taking models from experimentation to real production impact, and about the infrastructure that makes that possible at scale.

At Supercomputing Brabant and the TU/e Supercomputing Center I work on GPU workload optimisation and AI infrastructure for the SPIKE cluster, whose Spike-A extension runs on NVIDIA DGX B300. I help Brabant-based companies get their AI workloads running at scale. Before that, at Vencomatic Group, I built anomaly detection models, Azure data pipelines and dashboards for industrial automation, after a master’s thesis there that cut sorting errors in half.

As co-founder of LoopIt.ai, I led technical strategy and system architecture for an AI vehicle damage detection platform and mentored several cohorts of university interns. It won the EAISI AI Incentive Award.
