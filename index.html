About Me

Bridging machine learning research, high-performance computing, and real-world industrial impact.

I am a Machine Learning Engineer and Data Scientist with an MSc in Computer Science & Engineering from Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e). My focus centers on architecting production-grade machine learning pipelines, reinforcement learning, and neuromorphic computing systems designed for tangible economic and operational efficiency.

Currently at the TU/e Supercomputing Center, I contribute to Spike AI, engineering high-performance algorithms and workflows for Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) on supercomputer clusters. Simultaneously at Vencomatic Group, I design automated telemetry pipelines and Streamlit KPI dashboards monitoring international poultry production environments.

As Co-Founder & CTO of Loopit.ai, I spearheaded a 12-person team engineering an automated vehicle damage detection platform utilizing custom YOLOv8 deep learning models, earning the prestigious €1,000 EAISI AI Incentive Award.
