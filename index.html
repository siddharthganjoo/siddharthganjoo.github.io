Engineering Focus

I am a Machine Learning Engineer and Data Scientist with an MSc in Computer Science & Engineering from Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e). I specialize in distributed deep learning, high-performance computing on GPU clusters, and production computer vision.

At Supercomputing Brabant (TU Eindhoven), I work with research teams, startups, and high-tech partners to optimize and scale AI workloads on the Spike-A supercomputer. We lower the barrier to advanced supercomputing by migrating cloud bottlenecks to sovereign, distributed European compute.

Previously, I served as Co-Founder & CTO at Loopit.ai (directing computer vision models for vehicle inspection, earning the €1,000 EAISI AI Incentive Award), and engineered telemetry analytics pipelines at Vencomatic Group.
