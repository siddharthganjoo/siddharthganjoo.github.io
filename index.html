Introduction

About Me

Turning complex data into intelligent systems that drive real business impact

I'm a Data Scientist and ML Engineer with a Master's in Computer Science from TU/e, specializing in production-ready AI systems for agriculture, manufacturing, and automotive sectors.

My expertise spans deep learning (YOLO, CodeT5), reinforcement learning, and full-stack data pipelines. I've led the development of Loopit.ai, an AI-driven vehicle damage detection platform, and deployed RL-based calibration systems that save thousands of euros annually.

Currently at Vencomatic Group, I build interactive dashboards and data pipelines that transform poultry production analytics. As co-founder of Loopit.ai, I'm revolutionizing insurance claims automation.
