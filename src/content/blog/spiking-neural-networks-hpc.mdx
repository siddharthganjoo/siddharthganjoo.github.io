# Scaling Distributed AI Workloads on the Spike-A Supercomputer (AI-SIB)

Training and deploying state-of-the-art deep learning architectures requires compute capabilities far exceeding local workstations. The **AI Supercomputing Initiative Brabant (AI-SIB)**—a strategic collaboration between **TU Eindhoven (TU/e)**, **Tilburg University**, the **Province of North Brabant**, and **BOM**—democratizes access to high-performance computing power across the region.

## The Spike-A Supercomputer Architecture

At the heart of AI-SIB sits TU/e's **Spike-A supercomputer**. As a Machine Learning Engineer on Spike-A, my work focuses on:
- **Multi-Node Distributed Training**: Leveraging PyTorch Distributed Data Parallel (DDP) and Fully Sharded Data Parallel (FSDP) to train massive models without memory bottlenecks.
- **Compute Optimization**: Profiling CUDA kernels and optimizing data ingestion pipelines using Slurm batch scheduling to maximize GPU utilization.
- **Bridging Regional Innovation**: Assisting companies, researchers, and public institutions in scaling their AI workloads from local prototypes to production-grade supercomputing jobs.

## Strategic Path to EuroHPC

AI-SIB acts as the vital gateway connecting regional engineering teams with European supercomputing ecosystems, including **EuroHPC** and **AI Factory NL**, ensuring Brabant remains at the cutting edge of applied artificial intelligence.
